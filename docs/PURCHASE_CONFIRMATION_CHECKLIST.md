# Purchase confirmation verification

- Search a one-time service and open its live availability.
- Confirm price, stock, success rate and wallet balance are visible.
- If stock, price and wallet are sufficient, continue to review.
- Confirm the review shows product, country, activation type, final price, current balance and projected balance.
- Confirm the temporary-number warning is visible.
- Confirm the final purchase button is disabled and does not execute any request.
